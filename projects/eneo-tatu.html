PROJECT OVERVIEW

ICT infrastructure deployment for a commercial environment

The project involved the installation and deployment of ICT infrastructure supporting network connectivity, Wi-Fi, CCTV and telephony systems within a commercial environment.

The scope included structured cabling, infrastructure installation, equipment deployment and preparation of ICT systems for operational use.
