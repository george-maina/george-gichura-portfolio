Building and delivering reliable ICT infrastructure.

I am a Technical Engineer at HSC Systems Limited, with hands-on experience delivering network and ICT & ELV infrastructure across hospitality and commercial environments. My work spans network infrastructure, switching and configuration, structured cabling, Wi-Fi, fiber connectivity, CCTV, IPTV, telephony, testing and commissioning.

I combine field installation and troubleshooting with technical surveys, infrastructure assessment and project coordination. I support projects from site assessment and infrastructure planning through implementation, testing, commissioning and handover, working closely with project, design and sales teams to deliver practical technical solutions.
